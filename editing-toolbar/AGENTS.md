Its settings also keep `buttons`: the toolbar's buttons in order, `"|"` a separator between groups (left out: the
default set). Set it with `vau settings set plugin/editing-toolbar '{"buttons": [...]}'` or its settings sheet. The
buttons: `undo`, `redo`, `heading` (a menu of levels), `bold`, `italic`, `strikethrough`, `highlight`, `code`, `math`,
`comment`, `clear-formatting`, `internal-link`, `external-link`, `bullet-list`, `numbered-list`, `task-list`,
`indent`, `outdent`, `quote`, `callout`, `code-block`, `math-block`, `table`, `horizontal-rule`, `footnote`, `photo`.
