## Learning
The Learning page, a dashboard of `area` blocks (Logs), `books` and `week-goals`. Its areas: `reading` and `study`
(parent `learning`); a study log names its `subject` (Piano, Spanish, Statistics) and what it `focus`ed on; reading logs
may name the book (`book_id`) and `pages`. A vault may have its own areas for a subject (`music`), each its own `area` block.
