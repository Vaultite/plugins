## Gemini
The Gemini app's plan limits (Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra): how much of its 5-hour window and its week is used and
when each resets, read live from its Usage limits page (gemini.google.com/usage) in the agents' browser with the Google
sign-in there, once the user allows it (the `account` setting; `container`: another browser container's sign-in).
At most one read every 10 minutes, kept only in memory; nothing is written to the vault. `gemini_limits` says them;
`block-agent-limits` with `agent: gemini` shows them.
