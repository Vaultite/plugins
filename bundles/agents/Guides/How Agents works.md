Agents makes this vault the place your coding agents work from: their terminals beside your pages, what they cost and what they did, and the machines they run on.

## The pages
- [[Agents]]: your coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Hermes), their sessions and limits.
- [[Activity]]: what was done to the vault and by whom, you or an agent, and each day's recap.
- [[Server]]: the machine that runs Vaultite, how fast it is, and your other machines.
- [[Projects]]: each project in `Projects/`, its status and links.

## Working with agents
- **Terminals** are in the left sidebar: start an agent there, or hand it a note with Dispatch (⌘⇧↩) and it reads the note and does what it asks.
- Each terminal shows how much of its context the agent has used and how long its prompt cache lasts.
- **Machines** reach your other computers and agent VMs: their terminals and agents, from here or your phone.
- The right sidebar has your inbox and a live feed of what changed.

## Making it yours
Turn off the agents you don't use in Setup, Plugins, and pin the pages you open most. Settings, Restore previous setup puts your old setup back, and this guide is yours to edit or delete.
